About Tidal

An unusual arcade game: a submarine moves automatically and you control the water level around it. Raise the tide to submerge rocks, lower it to avoid floating mines, and collect pearls as you go deeper.

Title shown inside the game: TIDAL: Control the Ocean

How to play

  1. Press Dive.
  2. Drag up or down anywhere on the screen to raise or lower the tide.
  3. Raise the tide to cover rocks and lower it to avoid mines.
  4. Collect pearls and go as deep as you can. Smooth movements are safer.

Objective

Reach the greatest depth and collect pearls.

Controls

DesktopClick and drag up or down
MobileTouch and drag up or down

Features

  • Depth, pearls and best-depth counters
