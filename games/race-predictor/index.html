About Neon Ride

A lane-dodging bike game. You steer left and right on a highway while your speed increases. The run ends when you crash.

Title shown inside the game: Neon Ride: Highway Rush (Neon Bike Race)

How to play

  1. Press Start Engine.
  2. Steer left and right to avoid obstacles.
  3. Your score and speed are shown at the top.
  4. When you crash, press Ride Again.

Objective

Survive as long as possible for a high score.

Controls

DesktopLeft and Right arrow keys
MobileTap the left or right side of the screen

Features

  • Speed counter
  • Score and best score
