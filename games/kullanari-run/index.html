About Kullanari Run

An endless runner set in a village. Your character runs automatically and you time your jumps to clear obstacles. The score and your high score are shown at the top.

Title shown inside the game: Kullanari Runner: The Endless Village Runner

How to play

  1. Tap or press Space to start.
  2. Jump over each obstacle as it arrives.
  3. When you crash, tap or press Space to restart.

Objective

Run as far as you can and beat your high score.

Controls

DesktopSpace or Up arrow to jump
MobileTap the screen to jump

Features

  • Endless gameplay
  • Score and high score
  • One-button controls

Mobile

Yes. One tap to jump.
