About Queen Bee Kitchen

A tap-based cooking puzzle. Customer orders appear as cards. You select one and tap its ingredients in order at the Chef's Station. Orders expire, so speed and accuracy both matter.

How to play

  1. Tap an order card to select it.
  2. Tap the highlighted ingredients in the correct order.
  3. Finish dishes quickly to build combo bonuses.
  4. Do not let orders expire or your combo resets.

Objective

Complete orders to earn points, clear each level and beat your best combo.

Controls

Mouse or touchClick or tap order cards and ingredients

Features

  • Multiple levels
  • Combo bonuses
  • Game-over summary with score, level reached and best combo

Mobile

Yes. Built for tapping.
