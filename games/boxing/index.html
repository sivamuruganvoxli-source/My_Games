About 1v1 Boxing

CORNERMEN is a side-on boxing game played in rounds. You and your rival trade punches, and you win by reading the wind-up and dodging or jumping the shot. Rounds are timed and a match is best of three.

Title shown inside the game: CORNERMEN: Boxing

How to play

  1. Press Touch Gloves to begin.
  2. Work your footwork to close the distance.
  3. Punch when you are in range, and dodge or jump when your rival winds up.
  4. Win two rounds to win the match.

Objective

Win the best-of-three match.

Controls

MoveA and D / on-screen arrows
JumpW / Jump button
DodgeS / Dodge button
PunchSpace / Punch button

Features

  • Timed rounds
  • Combo counter
  • Jump and dodge moves

Mobile

Yes, but the game asks you to rotate your phone to landscape.
