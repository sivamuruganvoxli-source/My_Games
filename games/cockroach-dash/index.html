About Cockroach Dash

An arcade game set in a kitchen. You move a cockroach left and right, dash to safety and collect as many crumbs as you can before you get squished.

Title shown inside the game: Cockroach Man (kitchen survival)

How to play

  1. Press Start Game.
  2. Move left and right to collect crumbs.
  3. Use the dash to escape danger.
  4. When you are squished, the game shows how many crumbs you collected. Press Try Again to go again.

Objective

Collect as many crumbs as possible without getting squished.

Controls

DesktopLeft/Right arrows or A/D to move, Space to dash
MobileOn-screen left and right arrows

Features

  • Crumb counter
  • Dash move for close calls
  • Quick restart after each run

Mobile

Yes, with on-screen arrow buttons.
