About Race Predictor

A prediction game. You watch a live race track and decide whether the red car will overtake or fall back. Three difficulty levels are available. The game uses points, streaks and lives only. There is no money or betting.

Title shown inside the game: Race Predictor: Car Race Prediction Game

How to play

  1. Pick Easy, Medium or Hard.
  2. Watch the red car on the live track.
  3. Press Overtake or Fall Back to make your prediction.
  4. A wrong prediction costs a life. The game ends when all three lives are gone.

Objective

Build the longest streak and highest score.

Controls

Mouse or touchClick or tap the Overtake / Fall Back buttons

Features

  • Easy, Medium and Hard modes
  • Score, streak, lives and best score
