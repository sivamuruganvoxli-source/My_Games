About Chameleon

A fast color-matching survival game. Colored barriers slide toward your chameleon, and you must switch to the matching color before each one arrives. The game speeds up as your score grows.

Title shown inside the game: Meccha Chameleon: Color-Match Survival

How to play

  1. Press Start Game.
  2. Watch the color of the next barrier.
  3. Switch your chameleon to the same color before the barrier reaches you.
  4. Keep going as the speed multiplier rises. A wrong color ends the run.

Objective

Get the highest score you can. The game keeps your best score for the session.

Controls

DesktopArrow keys or number keys 1 to 4
MobileTap the color buttons

Features

  • Four colors to switch between
  • Speed multiplier that increases over time
  • Score and best-score display
