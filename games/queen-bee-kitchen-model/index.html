About Queen Bee Kitchen Model

Recipe Rush is a timed cooking game with a 60-second clock. The Queen Bee shows the recipe she needs and you tap matching ingredients. It is a separate, shorter game from Queen Bee Kitchen.

Title shown inside the game: Queen Bee Kitchen: Recipe Rush

How to play

  1. Press Start Cooking.
  2. Read the recipe and tap each ingredient it needs.
  3. Complete recipes to build a streak.
  4. When the 60 seconds are up, the game shows recipes completed and your streak.

Objective

Complete as many recipes as possible in 60 seconds.

Controls

Mouse or touchClick or tap ingredients

Features

  • 60-second timer
  • Recipe counter and streak counter
  • One-tap restart

Mobile

Yes. Built for tapping.
