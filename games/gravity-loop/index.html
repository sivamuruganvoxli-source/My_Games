About Gravity Loop

A one-button reflex game. Your ship circles an orbit and you switch its direction at the right moment to avoid asteroids and reach stars.

Title shown inside the game: Neon Orbit

How to play

  1. Press Play.
  2. Tap, click or press Space to reverse your orbit direction.
  3. Avoid red asteroids and touch gold stars.
  4. When you crash, press Try Again.

Objective

Collect stars, avoid asteroids and beat your best score.

Controls

DesktopClick or Space
MobileTap

Features

  • One-button control
  • Score and best score
