About Neon Arena P2P

An arena shooter that connects players directly (peer-to-peer). There is no public matchmaking: one player hosts and shares a room code, and the other pastes it to join. A local mode lets you test across two browser tabs on one computer.

Title shown inside the game: Neon Arena P2P: Online Multiplayer

How to play

  1. Choose Host Game and press Create Room, then send the room code to a friend.
  2. Your friend chooses Join Game, pastes the code, and sends back an answer code.
  3. Paste the answer code into Finalize Connection to start.
  4. Or choose Local Multiplayer to play across browser tabs on the same computer.

Objective

Score more kills than your opponent and avoid elimination. You respawn after being eliminated.
