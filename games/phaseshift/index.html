About PhaseShift

A one-button survival game. You exist in two phases at once: crimson obstacles are dangerous only in Phase A, and cyan obstacles only in Phase B. You shift phase to pass through whichever color is harmless.

Title shown inside the game: Phase Shift: Dimension Survival

How to play

  1. Press Enter the Rift.
  2. Tap, click or press Space to shift phase.
  3. Pass through obstacles of the color that cannot hurt you in your current phase.
  4. Survive as long as you can.

Objective

Survive as long as possible and set a new high score.

Controls

DesktopClick or Space
MobileTap anywhere

Features

  • Two-phase mechanic
  • Score and best score
