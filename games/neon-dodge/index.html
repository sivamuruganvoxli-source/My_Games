About Neon Dodge

A reflex arcade game. Red blocks fall or drift across the screen and you move your player out of the way while collecting gold stars.

How to play

  1. Press Start Game.
  2. Move to avoid the red blocks.
  3. Touch gold stars to score.
  4. Your best score is shown next to the current score.

Objective

Survive as long as possible and collect stars.

Controls

DesktopMouse or Arrow keys
MobileDrag with your finger

Features

  • Mouse, touch and keyboard support
  • Score and best score
