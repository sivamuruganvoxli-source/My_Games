About Tamil City

Tamil City is an open-world city-life game. You walk or drive an auto-rickshaw through districts such as a temple area, market, beach and harbor, talk to townsfolk, accept quests and earn coins. Menus are mostly in Tamil, with English help text in the Help panel.

Title shown inside the game: Tamil Nagaram (தமிழ் நகரம்)

How to play

  1. Choose New Game to start fresh, or Continue to load your saved progress.
  2. Walk around the city and talk to people to discover quests. Open the Quests panel to track them.
  3. Earn coins from quests, trading and mini-games, then spend them in shops or on gifts for friends.
  4. Open Pause and choose the save option to store progress. The game also autosaves periodically.

Objective

Complete quest lines, build friendships, earn money and unlock every achievement in the city.

Controls

MoveWASD or Arrow keys / on-screen joystick
SprintHold Shift
InteractE / round action button
MenusI inventory, Q quests, M map, Esc pause

Features

  • 14 townsfolk with schedules and dialogue
  • 13 quest lines and 20+ achievements
  • 8 shops with buying and selling
  • 6 mini-games including fishing, cooking and traffic dodging
  • Day and night cycle with weather
  • Save system and fast travel

Mobile

Yes. A joystick and action button appear on touch screens.
